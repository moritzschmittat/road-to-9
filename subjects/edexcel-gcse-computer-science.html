How to revise Computer Science with this list

  1. Rate every spec point honestly, from very weak to very strong.
  2. Revise the weakest topics first, one topic per session.
  3. Come back to each topic a day later, then a few days later, then a week or two later. Try to recall it before you look.
  4. From the spring, do full past papers and go back to the topics that cost you marks.
