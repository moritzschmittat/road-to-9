How it works

Three steps, then just follow the plan

Rate what you know

Go through your subjects and rate every spec point, from very weak to very strong. Be honest: the weakest topics come first.

Revise each topic once

Each day you get a short list of sessions, mostly 45 minutes. Start the timer, revise from your own notes, then rate the topic again.

Refresh it before you forget

Topics come back as 5 to 10 minute refreshers at growing gaps, so they stick until the exam instead of fading after a week.

Spacing study out and testing yourself are the two revision methods with the strongest evidence behind them. Read how Road to 9 uses them.

Every day

Open it, and your week is already planned

  • You set how long you can study each day, including holidays and days off.
  • Short on time or full of energy? Add or drop a session for today.
  • Mocks and exams pull refreshers forward, and the last four weeks before each exam put that subject first.
  • Past papers join the plan in the spring, and the topics that cost you marks come back sooner.
  • A journal and a to-do for each topic, waiting for you next time.

Questions

Good to know

Is Road to 9 free?

Yes. It is free for students taking their GCSEs in summer 2027.

How do I join?

Road to 9 is invite only for now. Every student has three invite links to pass on, so ask a friend who uses it. Or email hello@roadto9.com and tell us which school year and exam boards you are in.

Which subjects and exam boards does it cover?

16 subjects so far, including Edexcel International GCSE sciences, maths and languages, AQA humanities and OCR Latin and Classical Greek. The subjects page lists every board and course code. If yours is missing, tell us.

Does it replace my notes, textbooks or lessons?

No. Road to 9 plans what to revise and when. You still revise from your own notes, textbooks, revision guides and past papers.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. It runs in the browser on phones, tablets and laptops, so there is nothing to install. Your plan follows you between devices.

What happens to my data?

We keep your email, an optional first name and your planner. Nobody else can see your planner. No ads, no tracking, and you can download or delete everything in Settings.

Why "Road to 9"?

Grade 9 is the top GCSE grade. The plan is built to get every topic as strong as it can be before the exam.

Who makes Road to 9?

It started as a dad building a revision plan for his son's GCSEs. It worked well enough that his friends wanted it too, so it became Road to 9. It is run by Schmittat Ltd, a small UK company.
