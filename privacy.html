Privacy at Road to 9

In plain English. Last updated 13 November 2026.

The short version: we keep only what the planner needs to work, nobody else sees it, and you can delete everything yourself at any time.

Who runs Road to 9

Road to 9 is provided by Schmittat Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales (company number 09452378), registered office Aspen House, 107 Poyle Road, Farnham GU10 1DY. VAT number GB 976 4772 61. Schmittat Ltd is the data controller for your information. It is invite only. There are no adverts, no tracking, no analytics, and your data is never sold or shared for marketing.

What we store

  • Your email address, so you can sign in. We only use it to send your sign-in links.
  • The first name you type in Settings, if you type one.
  • Your planner: your subjects, your ratings for each topic, the sessions you've done, your journal notes and to-dos, your study times and exam dates.

Nothing else. We don't store your age, school, location or anything about your device.

Who can see it

  • Only you, when you're signed in. The database rules make sure each account can only read and change its own planner.
  • Not your parents, not other students and not your school. There is no parent view.
  • Schmittat Ltd, which runs the site, manages invitations on an admin page that shows account details only: your email address, first name, when you were invited, when a parent or carer agreed, when you last signed in and last used the planner, and whether you finished setup. It doesn't show your ratings, sessions, journal or to-dos.
  • Schmittat Ltd can technically access the stored data, as with any website. It does that for maintenance only and doesn't look at individual planners.

Where it's kept

Your data is stored with Supabase, in a data centre in London. The website itself is served by GitHub Pages. A copy of your planner is also kept in your browser so it works offline; signing out removes that copy.

Study groups (coming later)

If study groups are added, they will be invite-code only, and both sharing switches ("show when I'm studying" and "show topics I'm strong on") will be off until you switch them on. Your weak topics, percentages, hours, journal and to-dos will never be shared. Road to 9 does not provide any way to message other people.

Deleting your data

Settings → Account → Delete my account deletes your account and everything you've saved, immediately and for good. You can download a copy first with Export progress.

Your rights and questions

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data at any time (you can also delete it yourself in Settings). Email hello@roadto9.com. If you're unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).
